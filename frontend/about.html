01 —Career Story

My journey started with database and SQL development, before transitioning to Python backend work at GIS Consortium India in Noida — where I spent two years building REST APIs for e-governance platforms serving multiple municipal corporations across India.

Those APIs handled citizen-facing services: property tax, water billing, complaints — real impact at scale. I learned what "production" actually means when thousands of citizens depend on your endpoint being up at 9am.

I've since moved to full-stack freelance work, building complete platforms from scratch for clients — a live Bajaj dealership site with push notifications, WhatsApp API and Cloudinary CDN, a calisthenics business website, and real-time project management tooling. I also explore AI API integrations using OpenAI and Gemini inside Django backends.

Currently pursuing a Software Development certification at IIT Mandi covering Python, DSA, DBMS, OS, Networking, and GenAI — studying daily from 4am. The fundamentals matter.
